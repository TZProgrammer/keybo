# KITCHEN-SINK arm — result

**Does adding every feature keycraft and the other external projects have produce a BETTER MODEL?
No. The registered outcome is NULL**, which is what the pre-registration predicted (agreeing with the
parent's prior). Two of the arm's *own* predictions were refuted, and those are the valuable output.

Registered before any score: `KITCHENSINK-preregistration.md` (commit `3687e5e`). All tables
generated by `kitchensink_tables.py` into `KITCHENSINK_TABLES.md` — nothing hand-transcribed.

## Verdict on the three pre-registered criteria

| # | Criterion | bigram | trigram (primary) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Transfer: mean paired delta > 0 AND ≥3/4 folds sign-consistent | **FAIL** (+0.001995, 2/4) | **FAIL** (−0.002911, 0/4) |
| 2 | High-wpm non-regression, floor 80 (mandatory) | **PASS** (1/12 cells, 0 structural) | **FAIL** (6/12 cells, **0 structural**) |
| 3 | No ranking collapse, 15-name catalog | **PASS** (tau +0.9238) | **PASS** (tau +0.8476) |

Criterion 1 fails on both frames, so the arm is NULL regardless of the rest. Scope: 12 cells per arm
= 4 LOLO folds (azerty/dvorak/qwerty/qwertz — **not** the 15-name catalog) × 3 seeds; 2,202 bistroke
/ 16,643 tristroke rows.

## The bigram "win" is recovery, not new signal

Against the widened incumbent, bigram is +0.001995 (wilcoxon p=0.0771). Against the **narrow served
frame** it collapses to **+0.000499** (p=0.7334) — so roughly 75% of the apparent gain is regaining
ground the *direction* columns lost. Both p-values are optimistic anyway: the 12 cells are not
independent (4 folds × 3 seeds share folds; only 4 units are), which is why the registered rule is
fold sign-consistency rather than a p-value. The fold split is identical in transfer and in wmae —
azerty and qwertz gain, dvorak loses, qwerty mixed — so it is a per-fold property, not a per-feature
one.

## Two refuted predictions

1. **The structural dvorak b120 regression did not reproduce.** Predicted to recur and deepen a third
   time; measured at **1 of 3 seeds** on trigram (prior rounds: 3 of 3) and **positive on all three
   seeds** on bigram. **Zero structural sites in either frame** — every high-wpm failure is 1–2 of 3
   seeds, i.e. noise. "Adding columns deepens the structural regression" does not generalise; it was
   a property of the direction columns specifically.
2. **keycraft's whole one-hand-flow family is inert.** `onehand` (3RL) and `onehand_in` (3RL-IN) take
   **exactly zero total gain on all three seeds** — never split on once. The audit predicted
   recoverability (R² 0.5128 / 0.3200), not zero usage, so no cheap pre-screen could have found this.

## Two candidates rejected before costing a training round

* **`red_weak` (keycraft RED-WEAK) is bit-identical to `bad_redirect_sfgated`** on all 24,360 ordered
  triples (both fire on the same 432, zero differences). Its R² = 1.0000 against the widened frame is
  an *identity*, not an approximation: keycraft's RED-WEAK is REDIRGATE-1's gated bad-redirect under
  another name, and `sfgated-eval` already measured that column NULL. It reads as novel (R² = 0.685)
  only against the *narrow* frame, because the served `bad_redirect` fires 648× — 216 more. Pinned as
  a regression test.
* **`lsb_magnitude` (LSB-dist)** at R² = 0.9768 on the smallest support of any candidate (32 of 870).

## Hyperparameter tuning refuses to name a winner

`tune_lolo`, 16 candidates, kitchen-sink bigram frame. **Both registered guard predictions fired:**
`MarginTooSmall` (winner beats runner-up by **0.00305519** against a minimum-resolvable margin of
**0.0279988** — ~9× inside the resolvable band, so the honest answer is a TIE), and the tau gate
**gated nothing** (all 16 candidates at `tau_heldout = 1.0` — TAUGATE-1's saturated regime at full
grid size). At 4 folds the *selection* step has no resolving power over this grid. Trigram HPO not
run: UNSCOREABLE, not a pass.

## Version-locking

Additive under a third stamp, `FEATURE_VERSION_KITCHENSINK = "2026-07-05.3+kitchensink.1"`. Bigram
20→22→**27** columns, trigram 46→52→**69**. Nothing added to `_BIGRAM_PLACEMENT_NAMES` or
`_TRIGRAM_LEVEL_NAMES` — the shared prefixes of the version-locked served lists. **Narrow and widened
columns re-read by name out of the kitchen-sink matrix over the full enumeration: max abs diff
0.000e+00 in all four comparisons.** `is_scissor` still fires exactly 24. Suite: 1,025 tests, exit 0.

## Also fixed: `tune_lolo` could only tune the NARROW frame

It called `validate()` with no frame argument, so a widened or kitchen-sink arm had no way to request
its own hyperparameters — and comparing an untuned widened arm against a tuned narrow one puts the
confound in the **selection** step rather than the measurement. `direction`/`kitchensink` now thread
`tune_lolo → validate → train → features`, and through both model scorers.

## What generalises

Fifth consecutive feature-addition null on the same 4-layout frame (NGRAM-FE, ARM-M, direction ×2,
kitchen-sink). The 15 columns that *are* used take 7.15% of trigram gain and 8.28% of bigram gain —
**3.1× the direction round's 2.681%** — and still yield a negative mean trigram delta with zero
sign-consistent folds. Used-but-noise at three times the weight is the cleanest evidence yet that
**the binding constraint is the frame (4 training layouts), not feature count.** The registered
falsifier did not fire.

Corollary for future external-project audits: a "channel we lack" can be a column we already ship
(RED-WEAK) or one the model will not use at all (3RL/3RL-IN). Neither is visible from a feature list;
one enumeration and one training round settle both.
